Oddletics Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Oddletics (“the App”) handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Process

The App lets you draw competitors, name them, and enter them in virtual sporting events. Drawings, competitor names, competition results, records, and app settings associated with these features are primarily used to provide and save functionality on your device.

Unless a specific feature clearly states otherwise and requests any required permission, the App does not intentionally ask you to provide information such as your real name, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

2. Device Permissions

The App requests system permissions only when they are needed for a particular feature. The purpose of a permission will be presented through the applicable iOS permission interface. You can manage granted permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Refusing an optional permission will not affect unrelated core features.

3. How Information Is Used

Relevant data is used to provide App functionality, such as saving competitors you create, recording competition results and personal records, restoring app settings, and improving your in-app experience.

4. Sharing and Sale of Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not disclose personal information to third parties except where required by law, necessary to protect legitimate rights or safety, or necessary to provide a specific feature that you choose to use.

5. Storage and Deletion

Locally generated data is generally stored on your device. You may delete applicable content using deletion features provided by the App. Uninstalling the App will generally remove data stored in the App's local sandbox, although data retained by iOS, backup services, or copies you have exported may not be affected.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for the purpose of collecting children's personal information. If we become aware of circumstances requiring action under applicable law, we will take appropriate measures.

7. Third-Party Services

If a future version introduces analytics, crash reporting, cloud services, advertising, or other third-party services, we will update this Privacy Policy to reflect the actual data practices and provide any notices or permissions required by applicable rules.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information we process. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App's features, legal requirements, or data practices change. An updated effective date will be shown when a revised policy is published.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's data practices, please contact us using the developer contact information provided on the App's App Store product page.
